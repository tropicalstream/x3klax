# Changelog

## 1.0 — first release

KLAX (1990) remixed for the RayNeo X3 Pro.

- Ten waves, gentle opening curve, endless laps past wave 10.
- Solid bevelled tiles, ladder conveyor, particle bursts on every klax.
- Ten synthwave level tracks, ending on the MCP theme.
- Generated sound effects — saw/square/triangle oscillators, A-minor pentatonic.
- Three-gesture control: swipe a lane, tap to drop, double tap to flip.
